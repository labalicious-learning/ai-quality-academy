1. If you are not enrolled, read [course signup and calendar details](SIGNUP.md). Joining Discord does not reserve one of the cohort's 15 student seats.
2. Open https://discord.com/login or create your own account at https://discord.com/register. Use your own credentials; never share an account or password. Check the provider's current account eligibility with your instructor when needed.
3. Request the **Labalicious server invitation** through your existing private instructor contact. Invitations are issued privately by Jared; do not forward them. Learners cannot create server invitations. We do not publish an unrestricted invitation on this page. The channel links below open an existing membership; they are **not invitation links**.
4. Read **welcome-and-rules**. Jared assigns **Learner** after verifying enrollment privately. Until then, only the welcome channel is available. Do not post your email address, age or enrollment evidence in chat or public GitHub issues.

Already a member? Open the welcome channel:
https://discord.com/channels/1554532337591656559/1554542896256913439

If Discord says you cannot access a channel, check that you are signed into the invited account and have joined the server. If you see only welcome, ask Jared privately to check your enrollment and role. Do not create duplicate accounts to work around access restrictions.

## Voice and screen sharing on Mac, Linux and Windows

Start in a supported browser at https://discord.com/login. If you need the desktop app, use https://discord.com/download and select your operating system. Do not bypass school or employer installation restrictions; ask for an approved alternative.

Join **Office Hours**, check your microphone and speakers in Discord's voice settings, and mute when not speaking. Allow microphone or screen-capture access only when you intend to use it. On macOS you may need to approve screen recording in system privacy settings; on Linux the capture picker depends on your desktop/display system. Browser, OS and audio-capture support differ, so test your chosen setup before a session. If sharing fails, use a sanitized screenshot or a written description while a coach helps.

Share **one relevant application window** when possible. Close private tabs, hide notifications, and check the preview before sharing. A camera is not required for office hours. Do not screen-share credentials, personal records, private client work or another person's messages.

**Screen sharing is not remote control.** Discord's native screen sharing does not hand your keyboard or mouse to the viewer. For normal coaching, you remain at the keyboard and carry out the steps yourself. If hands-on remote control is necessary, Jared can arrange a separate approved tool and explicit, session-specific permission; it is not part of this Discord setup. Never enable unattended access or install a remote-access tool just because someone in chat asks you to.

Discord's official screen-sharing instructions:
https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040816151-Go-Live-and-Screen-Share

## Ask a useful, safe question

Use the [learner support guide](LEARNER_SUPPORT.md) for a full help template. Include your goal, expected result, actual result, OS/tool versions, what you already tried, and one small sanitized example. Keep each question's follow-up in its own thread.

Threads in course chat are visible to participants with access to that channel. They are **not private instructor conversations**. Assume anything you post could be copied or captured.

- Never post passwords, API keys, recovery codes, personal records or real client data. Check screenshots and public repositories before sharing.
- Be respectful. Ask before sending a private message; no unsolicited promotions or pressure to disclose personal information.
- No recording or transcription service is configured. Do not record or share another person's screen, voice or messages without permission. Channel permissions cannot prevent screenshots, recording or member-to-member DMs.
- Report access or safety concerns through your existing private instructor contact. Private feedback, accommodations and assessment decisions do not belong in course chat.
- Continue using the [submission workflow](SUBMISSIONS.md) for assessed work. A Discord post, reaction or role is not a submission, completion decision or certification award.
