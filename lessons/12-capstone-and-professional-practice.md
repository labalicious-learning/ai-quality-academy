# Lesson 12 — Product Showcase and Professional Practice

**Duration:** 90 minutes per showcase block; at most four individual presentations.
**Deck:** [Product showcase](../decks/12-capstone-and-professional-practice.md)
**Lab:** [Your final product presentation](../labs/12-defensible-qa-verdict.md)

## Outcomes

Learners demonstrate their own useful, visually appealing software; connect GitHub history, QA evidence and AI-assisted decisions; explain limitations and hand off a reproducible release. Jared Cluff records completed learning activities separately from demonstrated product quality and skills: **course completion** and **AIQAA certification** under [the two-award policy](../CREDENTIALS.md). No separate exam or surprise coding challenge.

## Instructor preparation

Apply [the 21-criterion rubric](../AIQAA_RUBRIC.md) to the frozen assessment agreement and release SHA. Complete the detailed evidence inspection before the showcase, keeping not-yet findings, unverified evidence and review-wide holds explicit. Use the published calibration and revision rules; no final-day hidden requirements, personal taste score or separate certification exam.

Use [Product Studio](../COURSE_PROJECT.md), [review checklist](../templates/project-review.md) and [showcase template](../templates/project-showcase.md). Review repositories and evidence before presentation day, including early/intermediate/test-fix/release history and a learner-explained issue-to-release chain; eight demo minutes cannot substitute for ongoing inspection. Use [the private certification review](../templates/aiqaa-certification-review.md) for the two distinct decisions. Confirm release SHAs, synthetic data, local fallback, accessibility arrangements and each learner's appointment.

Schedule four learners or fewer per block, each with eight minutes to demonstrate and four minutes to discuss the work with Jared. For larger cohorts book additional 90-minute blocks or individual appointments **with Jared present**. Parallel coach panels and team presentations cannot replace an individual's presentation to him. Additional showcase blocks extend delivery time for larger cohorts; the standard 19.5 scheduled live hours include one block.

Use isolated, credential-free environments for student code; no secrets or personal account access on demo machines. Prepare a private progress/decision register. Do not announce certificates automatically before Jared's review.

## What to discuss

Ask about decisions already represented in the product: who benefits, why a feature was cut, what a regression proves, what the model got wrong, what changed after review and what remains risky. This is a supportive conversation grounded in the learner's work, not an unseen test.

A polished UI alone is insufficient; neither is a passing CI badge on an unusable app. Look for the agreed working journey, visual/accessibility care, GitHub practice, business artifacts, verified AI use and safe recovery.

## Completion and support

Record **course complete / course activities outstanding** separately from **AIQAA certified / not yet demonstrated / review pending**. Completion recognizes finished learning activities and the presentation; certification requires all AIQAA-1.0 areas demonstrated through product quality, history and skills review. A learner with remaining product defects can complete the coursework and receive the completion certificate while working toward certification.

Only Jared authorizes each award. No numeric average or automatic issue from a bot. Record specific missing activities or certification gaps and arrange follow-up without repeating already demonstrated work. Use separate private certificate templates and issuance dates.
