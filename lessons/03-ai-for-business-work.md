## Learners will be able to

- turn a messy business question into a reviewable AI workflow;
- use AI with spreadsheets, documents, web research, GitHub, and team chat;
- preserve sources, formulas, decisions, and human approval; and
- recognize when a task needs a connector/tool, a structured dataset, or a human rather than a generic chat prompt.

## Instructor preparation

Use the five linked exports in Lab 3. Research sources are fabricated excerpts, not live pages. Recompute the budget instead of assuming CSV totals recalculate. Prepare a plain-text memo; connected-app work is an optional, separately approved extension.

**Live schedule:** Mondays, 7:30–9:00 p.m. Central (America/Chicago). Full labs and product implementation happen outside class.

## Agenda

| Time | Facilitation |
| --- | --- |
| 0–10 | Arrival: ask teams to list work they already do in Google Docs, Sheets, Discord, and GitHub. Mark where errors are costly. |
| 10–25 | Teach the business workflow canvas: source → transform → review → decision → record. Show why a pasted summary is not a system of record. |
| 25–40 | Demonstrate: clean a CSV without overwriting raw data, ask AI for anomalies, verify two calculations, and draft a cited one-page memo. |
| 40–50 | Mini-practice: turn a noisy chat thread into actions, decisions, assumptions, and unresolved questions. |
| 50–60 | Break. |
| 60–65 | Independent-lab handoff: open the linked resources and walkthrough, explain the deliverable and show where to save/submit it. Complete the full lab after class. |
| 65–80 | Review a prepared decision memo. The audience asks, “Where did this number/claim come from?” |
| 80–85 | Discuss appropriate automation: drafts and triage can be automated; external sends, payments, publishing, and data changes need approval controls. |
| 85–90 | Exit ticket: name one work process AI can accelerate and the proof/approval it still needs. |

## Independent lab — after class

Teams complete the operations sprint. Each member owns one surface: spreadsheet, docs/research, GitHub, or Discord. Budget about 35 minutes of focused practice; setup and troubleshooting may need additional time. Work solo or arrange a partner outside class. Use the lab’s linked resources and step-by-step walkthrough. Save the existing deliverables and use the [submission workflow](../SUBMISSIONS.md). Bring one verified result or a specific blocker to the next class; arrange help if you cannot finish. Product Studio homework remains separate.


## Teaching notes

This is the course’s broad-business lesson. Keep it practical: researchers cite sources; spreadsheet users preserve raw data and verify formulas; communicators separate quoted decisions from AI-generated suggestions; developers connect issues, diffs, and tests. Learners should leave seeing AI as an operating layer across work, not just a chatbot.

## Formative coaching

Teams submit a sourced decision memo, unchanged raw-data file, cleaned analysis, and a one-paragraph automation-boundary statement.
