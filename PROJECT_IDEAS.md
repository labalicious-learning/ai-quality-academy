# Twenty starting points for your Product Studio

Choose something you would enjoy using. These fictional-data ideas are not ready-made specifications. Pitch your choice using the [proposal](templates/project-proposal.md) and [scope rules](COURSE_PROJECT.md), or propose your own for instructor approval.

Every idea supports related records, state changes, a useful summary and import/export. Keep three core features. Stretch work waits until the main journey works well. None requires a paid model inside the product, real payments or real personal information.

## Make it yours without making it enormous

Choose a distinctive name, audience and visual direction. Specify one useful scenario, one situation to prevent and one failure to recover from. Replace “add AI” with a concrete benefit; a tested rules-based feature may be cheaper and more useful than a live model call.

For example, **Shelf Life** has items, loans and fictional borrower aliases. An item moves available → on loan → returned. Find an item, lend it, check the due list and return it. A duplicate loan must fail without corrupting records. CSV backup/restore provides an integration. Real email, identity accounts, payments and a mobile app are out of scope.
