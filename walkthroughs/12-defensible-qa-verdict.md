# Session 12 — Product Showcase, one step at a time

[Back to the showcase](../labs/12-defensible-qa-verdict.md) · [All help guides](../LAB_HELP.md)

**Finish with:** a presentation of your working product and the evidence of how you built it. Preparation uses your course-long homework. Your assigned slot is eight minutes to demonstrate and four minutes to discuss with Jared. There is no separate test.

## 1. Identify the version being reviewed

Open your own product repository, approved assessment agreement and [the project rubric](../AIQAA_RUBRIC.md). Record the full repository URL, release/tag and exact commit. Copy [the showcase template](../templates/project-showcase.md) into your existing product evidence area; do not create a second application or move code into the lab submission repo.

**Checkpoint:** your demo, tests and evidence refer to the same reviewed version. Mark any later change clearly and arrange review.

## 2. Assemble an evidence index

Link existing artifacts for approved scope, earlier/intermediate milestones, issue → branch → PR → human feedback → revision history, manual checks, a meaningful regression, visual/keyboard/mobile improvements, business/data work, recovery and AI/cost decisions. Link setup instructions and known limits.

Do not invent missing historical work. Identify gaps and discuss specific follow-up. Use the rubric and your agreed project scope to determine applicability; do not invent an account system solely to satisfy a hypothetical security example.

## 3. Rehearse the eight-minute demonstration

1. **0–1 minute:** audience, problem and three core features.
2. **1–4 minutes:** main journey, saved result, error/recovery and responsive/keyboard behavior.
3. **4–6 minutes:** one actual development chain and meaningful regression, with earlier history already reviewed.
4. **6–7 minutes:** affordable model use, a correction you made, spending and a business decision.
5. **7–8 minutes:** release, setup, known limits and next improvement.

Set a timer and rehearse with synthetic data. Open the exact tabs/files ahead of time. Notes are allowed. Practice explaining one decision in your own words for the following four-minute discussion.

## 4. Prepare for a connection problem

Keep a local working copy, safe screenshots and a short fallback recording. Show which behavior a fallback actually demonstrates; unobserved functionality remains unverified. Close private tabs, notifications and credential-bearing terminals before sharing. State supported/tested platforms honestly. Paid hosting and desktop presentation software are not required.

## 5. Save and check your work

Send the full repository URL and reviewed release through the instructor-approved private cohort channel. Confirm your presentation slot. An optional Session 12 packet is only an index/summary using [the submission workflow](../SUBMISSIONS.md). Keep personal feedback, identities and certificate decisions private.

[Course completion and AIQAA certification](../CREDENTIALS.md) are separate human decisions. A green packet bot or completed checklist does not award either one. Missing evidence gets specific follow-up on the project.

## If you get stuck

Choose the highest-priority evidence gap with Jared and agree on a concrete next action. If the app will not run, preserve the error and arrange follow-up; a polished slide cannot establish unobserved functionality. [Help checklist](../LAB_HELP.md).
