# Video guides for every lab

Start with **How to do a Labalicious lab**, then choose your session. These short, AI-narrated guides explain where to find the materials, how to approach the work, what to check, and how to finish. Pause and perform the steps yourself.

Every player includes English captions, chapter buttons, playback speed, a transcript, a video download, and direct resource links. Mac, Linux, and Windows differences are explained where needed. The written walkthrough remains the reference for exact commands and later updates.

## How to use the guides

1. Open the written lab and the linked resources alongside the video.
2. Choose the chapter you need. Pause to perform the step on your own computer.
3. Record your own observations; example screens in the video are not your submitted evidence.
4. Use the [submission workflow](SUBMISSIONS.md) when ready. Session 00 stays private; the final product remains in your own repository.

These public instructional guides contain fictional course material and AI narration. They are separate from classroom recordings and private learner feedback. Watching a guide does not automatically complete a lab or award certification.
