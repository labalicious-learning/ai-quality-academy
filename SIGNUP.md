## What happens next

1. Provide your preferred name, contact email, and agreement to receive course communications. Your GitHub username is optional; Session 00 helps you create an account.
2. The instructor reviews your signup. Submitting the form does not automatically approve enrollment.
3. Once enrollment is approved and session dates are confirmed, the course workflow sends a welcome email and Google Calendar invitations. Check your inbox and accept the invitations; they may not appear in your calendar automatically.

If Google asks you to sign in or the form is unavailable, contact your instructor through your existing private course channel. Do not post your email address or signup details in a public GitHub issue. A Google sign-in prompt alone does not confirm that your account has permission to respond.

## Keep private information private

Signup details are handled separately from the public course repository. Read the form's privacy and course-communications notice before submitting. Do not put personal details, passwords, credentials, private feedback, or real customer data in public coursework. Contact your instructor privately for signup corrections or withdrawal.

Course signup is not marketing consent. Signing up does not award a certificate: see [completion and certification](CREDENTIALS.md) for the separate requirements.
