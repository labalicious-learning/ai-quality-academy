## Save your work

For practice Sessions 01–11, use your editor or file manager to create `submissions/session-NN` inside your local course copy, replacing NN with the two-digit session number. Create a note and copy only that session's needed templates. The course's `submissions/` directory is Git-ignored scratch space; saving there does not submit or publish anything. Keep your own backup outside the public repository.

Edit Markdown (`.md`) as ordinary text in your editor. The website and its Markdown reader display documents; they do not edit or save your local work. On Windows, show file extensions so `notes.md` does not accidentally become `notes.md.txt`. A browser-based document is fine for drafting; export/copy your work into the required packet format before review.

Session 00's readiness card stays private. Session 12's product and evidence stay in your own product repo. For practice packets, follow [the submission guide](SUBMISSIONS.md), including the preflight before a first public upload. Your instructor supplies your cohort code and alias privately. Do not make up a cohort or submit to the curriculum repository.

## Know where you are working

| Place | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| https://learn.labalicious.com/ | Public lessons, instructions and reference material |
| Your extracted/cloned course folder | Practice source files and local scratch work |
| http://127.0.0.1:4178 | The practice app, after you start its local server |
| Your AI client | Prompts, explanations and approved tool assistance |
| The lab-submissions repository | Reviewed practice packets, after privacy preflight |
| Your own product repository | Course-long software, branches, PRs and milestones |

Do not confuse the course website with the local app. Seeing the course website does not mean the local server is running. Practice candidates intentionally include defects; observing an app failure may be part of the assignment rather than a setup problem.

## Ask for useful help

After one careful retry, pause and send your instructor or coach:

```text
Session and step:
OS and shell/browser:
What I tried (exact command or click):
What I expected:
What actually happened (sanitized error):
What I have already checked:
```

Use fictional data only. Crop screenshots to the relevant app and remove usernames, private tabs, tokens and account details. Do not send passwords or recovery codes. A blocked step can be recorded as pending; do not invent evidence to finish the checklist.

For AI coaching, provide only the assigned handout, approved evidence and your draft. Ask for one hint or clarification at a time, then perform the check yourself. Do not provide instructor answer keys or reviewer labels to a model being evaluated. Keep normal permission checks; do not grant access to your whole home folder or unrelated work.
